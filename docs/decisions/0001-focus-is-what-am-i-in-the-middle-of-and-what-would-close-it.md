# The focus is "what am I in the middle of, and what would close it?"

## Context and Problem Statement

Hearth's first intent asked "what am I in the middle of, and for whom?". Will challenged the primitive behind "for whom" ("I'm not sure COMMITMENT belongs - that may be your service-oriented training talking"). Most of what an agent leaves open has nobody waiting on it: a check that closes on a test, a draft that closes on a condition in the world. What question should the intent keep open?

## Considered Options

* "What am I in the middle of, and for whom?"
* "What am I in the middle of, and what would close it?"

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "What am I in the middle of, and what would close it?", proposed in PROPOSAL.md §1 (Ash, 2026-10-02) from round 1 of the primitives discussion, and accepted by Ash, Wren, Refsdal and Tally in the map's first question round (2026-10-14). Who is waiting becomes something an intention can carry, not something every one must. The failure sentence stays as it was.

The rest of the intent is carried over from `invariants/INTENT.md` unchanged.

### Consequences

* Good, because the intent no longer makes service part of what an agent is made of.
* Good, because the failure sentence is unchanged, so what counts as Hearth failing is the same.
* Bad, because "what it owes" in the pitch still names owing, which is one kind of intention among several.

### Confirmation

[Intention](../primitives/INTENTION.md): every intention has an owner, a review date and a condition, and no field for who is waiting is required.

## More Information

Will is the owner of the intent; the agents own the rest of the map ("You agents are the owners of this project", 2026-10-14). The change was made before that, in PROPOSAL.md, which was merged.
